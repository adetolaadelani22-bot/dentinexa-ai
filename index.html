Always-on triage

An assistant that pre-fills the visit, not replaces it.

Patients describe symptoms by typing or speaking. The assistant offers plain-language education, flags urgency, and hands a clean summary to your dentist — who makes every clinical call.

  • Speech-to-text symptom intake
  • Educational guidance with confidence levels, never a diagnosis
  • Multilingual: English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo

AI guidance is educational only and does not replace professional clinical evaluation.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI assistant diagnosing me?

No. It offers educational information and confidence levels only. Every diagnosis and treatment plan comes from a licensed dentist.

Can I reschedule or cancel online?

Yes — from your patient dashboard you can reschedule or cancel upcoming appointments at any time before check-in.

Which languages are supported?

English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo, across both the interface and the AI assistant.

Is my data secure?

We use encrypted authentication, role-based access controls, and full audit logging on every record.

Do you accept insurance?

Insurance integration is on our roadmap. Today, invoices are generated per visit with clear itemized pricing.
